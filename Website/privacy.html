Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Overview

This is the personal portfolio site of Maximilliano Felix (傅忠明). This policy explains what information is collected when you visit or use this site, and how it's handled. This site doesn't run any advertising, analytics tracking, or user accounts — it exists to showcase projects, skills, and experience, and to let visitors get in touch.

Information We Collect

Information you provide. If you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, subject, and message you enter. This is submitted voluntarily and used only to respond to you.

Information from third-party services. Some resources this site loads (see "Third-Party Services" below) may automatically receive standard technical data from your browser — such as your IP address and user agent — as part of how the web works. That data is handled by those providers under their own privacy policies, not by us.

Local storage. Your light/dark theme preference is saved in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

How We Use Your Information

Contact form submissions are used solely to read and reply to your message. We don't use your information for marketing, and we don't sell, rent, or trade it to anyone.

Third-Party Services

This site relies on the following third-party services to function:

  • Netlify — hosting and processing of contact form submissions. See Netlify's Privacy Policy.
  • Google reCAPTCHA — spam protection on the contact form. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — the Inter typeface, loaded from Google's servers.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) — the Remix Icon font used for icons.
  • CARTO / OpenStreetMap — map tiles shown in the Contact section.

Cookies

This site does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. Google reCAPTCHA may set cookies in your browser as part of its spam-detection process, governed by Google's own policies linked above.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained in Netlify Forms per Netlify's own retention practices. You can ask us to delete any information you've submitted at any time — see "Contact" below.

Your Rights

You can request access to, or deletion of, any information you've submitted through this site by emailing us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy
