Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Overview

This is the personal portfolio site of Maximilliano Felix (傅忠明). This policy explains what information the site collects when you visit or use it, and what I do with it. The site has no ads, no analytics tracking, and no user accounts. It's here to show my work and to give you a way to contact me.

What I Collect

Information you provide. If you send a message through the contact form, I receive the name, email address, subject, and message you enter. If you request a meeting, I receive your name, email address, preferred date and time, and whether you want a video or phone call. You choose to send this, and I use it only to reply to you.

Information from third-party services. Some resources the site loads (listed under "Third-Party Services" below) receive standard technical data from your browser, such as your IP address and user agent, because that's how loading anything on the web works. Those providers handle that data under their own privacy policies. I don't receive it.

Local storage. The site saves your light/dark theme choice in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and the site never sends it anywhere.

How I Use It

I read your message or meeting request and reply to it, or set up the meeting you asked for. I don't use your information for marketing, and I don't sell, rent, or trade it to anyone.

Third-Party Services

The site depends on these services:

  • Netlify - hosting and processing of contact form and meeting request submissions. See Netlify's Privacy Policy.
  • Google reCAPTCHA - spam protection on the contact form. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts - the Inter typeface, loaded from Google's servers.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) - the Remix Icon font used for icons.
  • CARTO / OpenStreetMap - map tiles shown in the Contact section.

Cookies

The site doesn't set any tracking or advertising cookies of its own. Google reCAPTCHA may set cookies in your browser to check for spam, under Google's privacy policy linked above.

Data Retention

Netlify Forms stores contact messages and meeting requests according to Netlify's own retention practices. You can ask me to delete anything you've sent at any time, using the email address under "Contact" below.

Your Rights

You can ask to see, or have me delete, any information you've sent through this site. Email me at the address below.

Children's Privacy

The site isn't aimed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest revision.
